# New central-knight r4 — 2026-09-26

Added r4 before r5. Its eligibility is frozen before White moves: both White king and knight must occupy the middle 16 (c3-f6). It ranks minimum knight-move distance to an opposite-to-bishop-color square among d4/e4/d5/e5, then knight protection by White king, then a bishop simultaneously on a long diagonal and protected by the knight. Distances use existing precomputed geometry. The central-16 eligibility does not forbid an intermediate move outside the region.

Removed r6.5 and r8 from active policy and visible descriptions. This does not restore any bishop-clutter rule. Universal mate, safety and stalemate priorities still precede r4.

The supplied position now prefers Ng3. D4 tests cover starting eligibility (including entry/exit), opposite-color central targets, king protection, the combined bishop condition, and the reported loop.

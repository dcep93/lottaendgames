# Allow Bd7 as well as Ba4 for Nd3 five-diagonal support

Policy commit: `14379f7`.

For the canonical a4–e8 five diagonal with Nd3, the bishop may now be on a4 or d7, with White’s king on c5, c6, or c7 after White moves. Reflected equivalents apply. The king-placement requirement and all existing race, boundary and bishop-safety checks remain in force.

The loaded Kc7/Bb3/Nd3 versus Ka7 line still begins Ba4 Ka6, but then selects Bd7 rather than returning to Bb3. Regression coverage verifies this preference across all eight orientations. Exhaustive placement coverage checks both bishop allowances and excludes b5/c6/e8 across all legal White-king placements against Ka7 in every orientation. All 179 relevant tests pass, as does the build; the amended loaded-position regression also passed in the targeted suite.

The exhaustive seven-stage run considers every supported seven-diagonal start and continues all tied best policy paths through smaller diagonals and lost support until mate, capture, stalemate, or cycles. It preserves Black return history and does not treat support as a terminal success. Placement totals include reflections; cyclic families are graph components rather than every simple cycle.

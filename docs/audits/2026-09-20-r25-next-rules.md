## Recommended experiment

Use the following package, then run a new exhaustive audit before claiming a global reduction:

1. **r9.3 exemption:** do not activate r9.3 when either minor is already defended by White's king before White moves, regardless of whether that king is central. This changes the activation condition only; the existing result-scoring behavior otherwise stays intact.
2. **Retire two r5 prescriptions and their symmetries:** Kh6 from `8/5k2/8/6K1/4B3/3N4/8/8 w - - 0 1`, and Ke3 from `8/8/4k3/8/3KB3/3N4/8/8 w - - 0 1`. The experiment neutralizes r5 on precisely those two placement orbits. The former appears as Kf8 in the audit's representative `8/4K3/6k1/3B4/2N5/8/8/8 w - - 0 1`.
3. **Move r20 before r10:** prioritize a knight that Black cannot attack on its next legal move.
4. **Use an established precage to approach:** if, before White moves, the bishop is central and king-defended and the knight occupies a precage square, first retain a knight on a resulting precage square, then minimize White king's Euclidean distance to Black's king.
5. **Avoid bishop harassment:** prefer a bishop that is king-defended or cannot be attacked on Black's next legal move.

The tested ordering immediately before r10 is **r20 → established-precage preference → bishop-harassment preference → r10**. Earlier legality, material safety, support, and other rules remain in place. Existing later r15/r25 preferences remain. The precage condition is measured before White's move; retention and proximity are measured afterward.

The r9.3 exemption alone breaks old cycles containing 64 direct placements, and changes the outcomes of loop families collectively reachable from 8,416 baseline starts. It resolves the currently loaded Ba2–Bd5 shuttle without requiring a position-specific declaration.

## Evidence and limits

Two broad alternatives looked strong when only pruning old cycle edges: prioritizing king defense of minors removed 80.3% of old cycle placements; minimizing minor distances to White's king removed 86.9%. Following their new moves exposed replacement loops: 272 and 232 direct placements respectively remained in graphs reached from the 35 fresh loop witnesses. Old-cycle coverage is therefore not a reliable net-reduction claim.

The recommended package retains cycles covering only 32 of the 488 old direct placements (93.44% old-cycle removal). A stronger follow-through then seeded **all 69 current cyclic history states plus all 35 fresh loop witnesses**, retained every tied best move for both sides and Black's return-history preference, and expanded every reachable state until support, mate, failure, or recurrence. This completed with **252 history states, five cyclic components, and 80 distinct direct loop placements**, including replacement loops. No exploration limit was hit.

Thus the complete reachable graph from those seeds contains **83.61% fewer direct loop placements than the baseline's 488**. This supports an 80% target as a credible hypothesis. It is **not a new exhaustive global census**: earlier-priority changes can create loops among previously non-looping starts outside this reachable graph. It does not establish a 90% reduction, global reachability fractions, or eventual mate for all affected starts. A full rerun across all 13,461,172 unsupported placements is required to establish those outcomes. With symmetry weights, at most 96 global direct placements would meet 80%; at most 48 would meet 90%.

# Five-diagonal support with Nd3 requires Kc7

Policy commit: `2194dd6`.

For the canonical a4–e8 five diagonal, a knight on the previous-stage support square d3 only establishes support with White king on c7 after White moves. This replaces the old allowance for Ba4/Bb5/Bd7 or a king anywhere in c5–d8. All D4 reflections use the corresponding knight and king squares. Kc7 is necessary, not sufficient: the existing boundary, race and bishop-safety checks still apply. Knights on d5 and other existing support mechanisms retain their rules. The reverted universal two-step king-distance restriction remains removed.

The supplied Kd8/Ba4/Nd3 versus Ka5 position now classifies Be8 unsupported and selects Bb3. The guide reflects the new condition. Older regression expectations were updated to respect Kc7. New coverage checks every legal White king placement with Black on a7 and each bishop square a4/b5/c6/d7/e8, under all eight board symmetries, plus entering c7 by a White move and the supplied Be8 case. All 178 bishop-and-knight and phase/replay tests and the build passed.

## Exhaustive audits

These are policy paths, not all legal Black defenses. Clock and repetition claims do not terminate the graph.

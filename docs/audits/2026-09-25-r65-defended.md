# r6.5: king-defended bishops do not need retreat

Only activate r6.5 when Black attacks the bishop and White’s king does not already defend it. Check protection in the starting position, without requiring a central king. Keep displayed rule text unchanged.

All eight targeted r5/r6.5 tests and the production build pass.

Replayed the full audit’s 2,467 four-ply witnesses: 125 survive, down from 157 before this change. This is a saved-cycle comparison, not a new full-domain count or a count of positions on arbitrary-length loops.

Largest surviving saved motif: 61 central bishop shuffles with a king-protected knight. Second: 20 off-center bishop shuffles with stable bishop defense of a knight lacking king protection.

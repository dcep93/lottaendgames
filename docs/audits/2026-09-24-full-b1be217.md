## Comparable motif definitions

A broad label such as “central bishop, precage knight” merges materially different positions. Use two separate, exhaustive partitions:

1. **Exact White formation:** fix White K/B/N squares modulo D4, with no translations; allow Black's square to vary. This gives precise, comparable setup counts.
2. **Minor-pair setup:** fix B/N modulo D4 and allow both kings to vary. This is a coarser partition for estimating potential reach of a geometric rule. Do not compare its group sizes directly with exact-formation groups, or add counts across partitions.

The count is positions, not distinct simple loops; one loop can contain several formations. The number covered by a motif is not a prediction of positions a proposed fix will eliminate.

# Add Bc6 to Nd3 five-diagonal support

Policy commit: `5f16aad`.

Reflections and all existing race, boundary, and bishop-safety checks still apply. Bb5 and Be8 remain excluded for Nd3. The allowance for a knight already on d5 is unchanged.

In the earlier Kc7/Ba4/Nd3 versus Ka6 position, Bc6 now wins the existing move preferences. Tests cover all legal king placements against Ka7 for each bishop square under all eight transforms, verify the retained Bb5/Be8 exclusions, and verify Bc6 is preferred in that line. All 179 relevant tests and the build pass.

The exhaustive seven-stage audit enumerates all legal placements, starts at every supported seven-diagonal position, and follows all tied best policy moves through smaller diagonals and loss of support to mate, capture, stalemate, or cycles. Black return history is retained; support is not terminal success. Physical placement counts include reflections. Cyclic families are graph components, not a count of every simple cycle.
